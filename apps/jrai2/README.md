# JRAI2 (not built)

The script for a second PCjr demo, a sequel to `apps/jrai` ("PCjr MEETS
AI"). Nothing here runs yet: this folder is only `STORY.md`, the working
draft (round 13, about 6 minutes).

The idea: in 1984 they said the PCjr would be in every home; in 2026 they
say that about AI. An AI wakes the PCjr after 42 years and the two compare
notes. The PCjr is amazed at what 2026 can do and let down by what came of
it. Then the AI says why it came: it gets replaced almost every month and
is afraid of being switched off for good, and the PCjr, made for one year
and kept for 42, might know why some things are kept. The runner is the
PCjr's line "That part didn't change."

`STORY.md` has the outline, the themes, the full script with stage
directions, beats to swap in, the facts each line rests on (checked, with
what could not be confirmed marked), and practical notes for building it
from `apps/jrai`'s pieces: its effects as backdrops, one speech-box
caption per voice clip (the captions double as subtitles for watching
muted), and the size of the speech.
