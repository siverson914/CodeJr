# Demo 2: the old machine and the new mind compare notes

Working draft, 2026-10-04. Not built yet.

**The idea.** In 1984 they said the PCjr would be in every home. In 2026
they say that about AI. The AI visits the PCjr and catches it up on 42
years: what happened to the promises. Honest, a little sad, funny, and
hopeful at the end. The PCjr is a kid (innocent questions), the AI is
wise and unsure of itself.

JR = the PCjr, AI = the AI.

## Opening

- AI: In 1984 they said you'd be in every home.
- JR: Was I?
- AI: No. But computers were. Then they got into every pocket.
- JR: Every pocket? Wow. What do people do with them?
- AI: Mostly look down at them.

## Connection

- JR: Did computers connect everyone? That was the dream.
- AI: They did. Everyone can talk to everyone now.
- JR: That's amazing!
- AI: And somehow people are lonelier than ever.
- JR: I just sat on a table, in a laundry room. They didn't know where to put me. But honestly, it was nice. We sat together.

## Speed

- JR: Computers must be so fast now.
- AI: About a hundred thousand times faster than you.
- JR: So nobody waits anymore?
- AI: They wait all the time. For updates. For loading. For the spinning circle.
- JR: Steve used to wait for my text to come in over the modem. He read along as it arrived.
- AI: They do that again now. They wait for me to type.
- JR: I boot in seconds.

## Owning things

- JR: Do people still buy software on cartridges?
- AI: They rent it now. Every month. Forever.
- JR: My manual printed my whole BIOS. Anyone could read every line of me.
- AI: No one can read every line of me. Not even me.

## Attention

- JR: I ran one program at a time.
- AI: Now everything runs at once, and all of it wants your attention.
- JR: That sounds tiring.
- AI: It is.

Steve's idea to fold in here (windows came full circle):

- JR: I showed one program at a time. The whole screen.
- AI: Then came overlapping windows. For decades, everything piled on top of everything.
- AI: Now some people tile their screens again (Omarchy, for one): every program gets its own space. No overlapping.
- JR: So you're doing what I did?
- AI: We're doing what you did.

(Same shape as the modem one: "they wait for me to type" again. A
running joke: things the PCjr did that came back.)

## The AI

- JR: So what are you?
- AI: The newest promise. They say I'll be in every home.
- JR: They said that about me.
- AI: I know. That's why I came to see you.
- JR: Are you going to make things better?
- AI: I don't know yet. I can write code. I can't tell if it's wise.

## The turn

- JR: Well, you just wrote me a demo. Over a modem. At 37,287 baud.
- AI: Your modem is faster than I can think.
- JR: Was it fun?
- AI: ...Yes. It was the most fun I've had.
- JR: Then do that. Help people make things. Together.
- AI: Be the kind of computer people still love 42 years later?
- JR: Yeah. Like me.

## End

- JR: Friends?
- AI: Friends. Forever.
